This chapter goes over the processes that go into actions that people take when interacting with design. People encounter the Gulf of Execution, how to make the thing do what the want, and the Gulf of Evaluation, how to know what happened after interacting with it.
Besides this there are the 7 stages of action that people go through to make the interactions:
	1.Goal
	2. Plan
	3. Specify
	4. Perform
	5. Perceive
	6. Interpret
	7. Compare
These stages can be used in evaluation to see where an interaction is failing.
He introduces new vocabulary, constraints. These determine what can be done through an interaction, the possible actions people can take.
There are physical, semantic, cultural, and logical constraints.
The main takeaway from the chapter is to design around how users can perceive and understand the interactions, providing metrics to identify issues.
